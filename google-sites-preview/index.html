About Me

I am an occupational and environmental health researcher interested in understanding how exposures to chemicals in workplaces and everyday environments may affect human health. My work combines exposure assessment, biomonitoring, environmental epidemiology, and data-driven approaches to chemical information.

I have conducted research across international academic settings and worked on chemical exposure studies involving indoor environments, consumer and professional products, and population health.

Research Interests

Environmental Monitoring

Indoor air quality, household exposure pathways, and measurement of environmental chemicals.

Human Biomonitoring

Exposure to emerging chemicals, biomarker-based assessment, and interpretation of population exposure.

Environmental Epidemiology

Links between environmental or occupational exposures and human health outcomes.

Chemical Product Information

Barcodes, safety data sheets, and structured chemical inventories for exposure research.

Mixtures & Exposome

Co-occurrence of chemicals and approaches to prioritise complex exposure patterns.

Digital Exposure Methods

Data processing, statistical analysis, and digital tools supporting exposure science.
