About Me

I am an occupational and environmental health researcher interested in understanding how exposures to chemicals in workplaces and everyday environments may affect human health. My work connects chemical exposure assessment, the exposome and mixture exposures, human biomonitoring, and AI-assisted methods for more informative environmental and occupational health research.

My research experience spans Karolinska Institutet, University College Dublin, ISGlobal Barcelona, and the University of Manchester, encompassing indoor air quality, pesticide biomonitoring, chemical mixtures, consumer products, and drinking-water exposures.

Research Interests

Chemical exposure assessment · Exposome & mixtures · Digital exposure science

Environmental Monitoring

Indoor air quality, household exposure pathways, and measurement of environmental chemicals.

Human Biomonitoring

Exposure to emerging chemicals, biomarker-based assessment, and interpretation of population exposure.

Environmental Epidemiology

Links between environmental or occupational exposures and human health outcomes.

Chemical Product Information

Barcodes, safety data sheets, and structured chemical inventories for exposure research.

Mixtures & Exposome

Co-occurrence of chemicals and approaches to prioritise complex exposure patterns.

AI-Assisted Exposure Assessment

Digital exposure assessment, large language models, and chemical product information to improve exposure research.
